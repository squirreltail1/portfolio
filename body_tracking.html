SoundField

  • Created SoundField, an interactive system that turns physical space into a collaborative six-string guitar using real-time camera tracking and projection.
  • Pioneered a social and embodied approach to ear training, making musical skill development more accessible and enjoyable for users of all experience levels.
  • Engineered the application in JUCE, implementing a physical model for authentic guitar synthesis and leveraging the ZED SDK for robust multi-person motion tracking.
  • Validated through user testing that the collaborative environment significantly improved performance and reduced frustration for less experienced participants compared to solo, traditional methods.
