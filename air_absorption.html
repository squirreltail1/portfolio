Air Absorption Simulation In Real Time - Unity

  • Implemented the ISO 9613-1 air absorption model in C# as a real-time audio plugin for Unity, simulating frequency-dependent sound attenuation over distance.
  • Used FFT convolution with an overlap-add method to apply a dynamically calculated impulse response based on temperature, humidity, pressure, and source-listener distance.
  • Designed a Unity environment with a customizable GUI to adjust atmospheric parameters in real-time and visualize the effect of air absorption on sound.
  • Validated the model by comparing its magnitude response with analytical results, confirming accurate attenuation behavior, especially in low-pass filtering effects over distance.
